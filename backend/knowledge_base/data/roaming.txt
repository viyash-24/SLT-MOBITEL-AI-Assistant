For postpaid subscribers, SLT-MOBITEL international roaming can be activated by dialling #999# or using the SLT-MOBITEL Selfcare App. A standard voice and data roaming activation requires a LKR 10,000 deposit. The Singapore unlimited data roaming booster costs USD 5 per day.

For prepaid subscribers, activate SLT-MOBITEL roaming by dialling #999# and selecting Prepaid Roaming Activation. No deposit is required, but the account should maintain a minimum balance of LKR 1,000. After landing, turn on data roaming; the device can connect to Singtel or StarHub in Singapore.
